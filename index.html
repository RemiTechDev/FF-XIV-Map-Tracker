HOW TO USE

Guide

A quick walkthrough of the tracker, statistics and backups.

QUICK START
1Choose a map
2Enter results
3Save session
4Check statistics
5Create a backup
01

Select a Treasure Map

Choose the map you are running. The app automatically knows its level, type and whether it can open a treasure dungeon portal.

02

Enter the session results

Record completed maps, opened portals, dungeon clears, earned Gil and the total purchase cost of the maps.

03

Read the statistics

Portal rate is calculated only from portal-eligible maps. Clear rate is calculated from opened portals. Profit equals earned Gil minus map purchase cost.

04

Export & restore

Use JSON for a complete restorable backup, CSV for raw session data, XLSX for a polished report and ODS for LibreOffice Calc.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What counts toward portal rate?

Only completed maps marked as portal eligible in the map database are used as the denominator.

What is net profit?

Net profit = Gil earned − total cost of purchased maps.

Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored in this browser using localStorage. Export a JSON backup before clearing browser data or moving to another device.

What happens to older V3 data?

The app migrates existing V3 totals into legacy sessions. Their original dates and historical map costs were not stored, so time-based charts exclude entries without a known date.
